# Abiturkanon

Analysis of the German school-leaving exam ("Abitur") reading canon: which literary texts are set for Abitur exams across Germany's 16 federal states, how that list has changed over time, and how it relates to each text's presence in the book market (editions and translations held by the German National Library, DNB).

The analytical framing borrows tools from ecological modelling---texts are treated as "species" that colonise and go extinct in different states over time---to study how texts enter, spread, and drop out of the canon, and how "sticky" (durable) that canon status is.

## Data

- `data/260727_dnb_results_manuallyFiltered.csv` — raw DNB export (source data).
- `data/metadata_abitur.csv` — raw Abitur reading-list data, one row per text/state/year (source data).
- `data/overview_abitur.csv` — state × year table of available reading lists (source data). Used for the map and heatmap, and to distinguish missing lists from absent titles in all dynamic analyses.
- `data/dnb_detail.csv` — cleaned DNB catalogue entries for texts in the dataset (title, author, publication year, language, series); generated by the pipeline.
- `data/dnb_with_abitur.csv` — DNB entries merged with Abitur reading-list data, classified by literary period (Classic vs. Contemporary) and entry type (edition vs. translation); generated by the pipeline.
- `data/title_summary.csv` — one row per text, summarising Abitur coverage (states × years) alongside DNB edition/translation counts and coverage-resolution status; generated by the pipeline.

## Analysis pipeline

`code/DNB_SRU.ipynb` runs the search-retrieval logic for the titles in the DNB catalogue.

`code/abiturkanonAnalyses.R` runs end to end and produces all files in `analyses/` and `img/`. It is organised in seven numbered sections:

1. **Setup** — packages, plot theme, paths and constants.
2. **Reading-list data and coverage** — map and heatmap of coverage, long-format data, a coverage mask (which state-years have a list) and the balanced panel.
3. **Bibliographic data** — cleaning of DNB records, classification into editions and translations, and the correlation between Abitur coverage and DNB coverage.
4. **Descriptive overview** — text- and author-level summaries, heatmaps, share of female authors.
5. **Canon dynamics** — Shannon diversity, partitioned into within-list and between-state diversity; titles added to and removed from each list per year; within-state and canon-wide turnover (Jaccard/Sørensen); colonisation rates per title (Levins, rescue-effect variant); contagion gap per year.
6. **Change-point tests** — whether 2026 deviates from the 2006–2025 trend (95% prediction interval), and an exploratory segmented regression for a change from 2020.
7. **Persistence** — survival spells, Kaplan–Meier curve and Cox proportional-hazards models with time-varying covariates (lagged colonisation rate, cumulative DNB editions and translations), plus sensitivity models.

### Handling of missing data

Reading lists are available for 159 of 336 state-years. A missing state-year is treated as unobserved, not as an empty list:

- year-to-year transitions are only counted where a state has a list in both years;
- survival spells followed by a year without a list are right-censored; spells that begin directly after such a year are flagged as having an unknown start;
- titles only enter the analysis from their year of publication.

Key analyses are repeated on a balanced panel of the seven states with a list in every year from 2013 to 2026 (BW, HB, HH, HE, NI, NW, SN).

## Outputs

### `analyses/`
- `text_summary.csv`, `author_summary.csv` — text- and author-level frequency summaries.
- `shannon_diversity_by_year.csv` — pooled, within-list and between-state Shannon diversity per year.
- `period_rates_titles.csv` — titles added and removed per list, list length and within-state turnover per year.
- `turnover_time_series.csv` — canon-wide turnover between consecutive years.
- `metapop_rates_titles.csv` — colonisation and extinction rates per title.
- `period_contagion_titles.csv` — contagion gap per year.
- `break_2026_tests.csv`, `break_2020_segmented_tests.csv` — change-point tests.
- `spells_titles.csv` — survival spells (title × state).
- `cox_model_coefficients.csv` — main Cox model; `cox_models_all.csv` — all model specifications.
- `*_balanced.csv` — the same measures for the balanced panel.

### `img/`
- `map_states.png` — Abitur text coverage by federal state (East Germany hatched).
- `heatmap_data.png` — state × year data availability.
- `hist_DNB_editions.png`, `hist_DNB_translations.png`, `scatter_DNB.png` — DNB edition/translation distributions and their correlation.
- `scatter_DNBxAbi.png` — Abitur coverage vs. DNB coverage.
- `author_heatmap.png`, `text_heatmap.png` — canon presence over time for recurring authors/texts.
- `gender_proportion.png` — share of female authors over time.
- `shannon_diversity_by_year.png`, `shannon_partition_by_year.png` — Shannon diversity over time, pooled and partitioned into within-list and between-state diversity.
- `turnover_time_series.png`, `turnover_within_state.png` — canon-wide and within-state turnover.
- `period_rates.png` — titles added to and removed from reading lists per list and year.
- `period_contagion.png` — contagion gap per year.
- `km_survival_curve.png` — Kaplan–Meier curve for canon-membership "spells".
