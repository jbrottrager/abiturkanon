# Abiturkanon

Analysis of the German school-leaving exam ("Abitur") reading canon: which literary texts are set for Abitur exams across Germany's 16 federal states, how that list has changed over time, and how it relates to each text's presence in the book market (editions and translations held by the German National Library, DNB).

The analytical framing borrows tools from metapopulation ecology — texts are treated as "species" that colonise and go extinct in different states over time — to study how texts enter, spread, and drop out of the canon, and how "sticky" (durable) that canon status is.

## Data

- `data/260727_dnb_results_manuallyFiltered.csv` — raw DNB export (source data).
- `data/metadata_abitur.csv` — raw Abitur reading-list data, one row per text/state/year (source data).
- `data/overview_abitur.csv` — state × year Abitur presence table, used for the map and heatmap (source data).
- `data/dnb_detail.csv` — cleaned DNB catalogue entries for texts in the corpus (title, author, publication year, language, series); generated by the pipeline.
- `data/dnb_with_abitur.csv` — DNB entries merged with Abitur reading-list data, classified by literary period (Classic vs. Contemporary) and entry type (edition vs. translation); generated by the pipeline.
- `data/title_summary.csv` — one row per text, summarising Abitur coverage (states × years) alongside DNB edition/translation counts and coverage-resolution status; generated by the pipeline.

## Analysis pipeline

`code/DNB_SRU.ipynb` runs the search-retrieval logic for the titles in the DNB catalogue.
`code/abiturkanonAnalyses.R` runs end to end and produces all files in `analyses/` and `img/`. Broadly, it:

1. Cleans and merges the DNB catalogue and Abitur reading-list data.
2. Maps state-level Abitur presence over time (choropleth and presence/absence heatmap).
3. Summarises text- and author-level frequency in the canon, including a gender-representation trend.
4. Checks the correlation between a text's Abitur coverage and its market presence (DNB editions/translations).
5. Models canon dynamics using metapopulation statistics: colonisation and extinction rates per text and per year, contagion dependence (whether adoption in one state predicts adoption elsewhere), corpus-wide turnover (Jaccard/Sørensen similarity between consecutive years), and Shannon diversity of the yearly canon.
6. Models "stickiness" (how long a text persists once it enters the canon) via Kaplan–Meier survival curves and Cox proportional-hazards models, including a time-varying (age-period) model that uses cumulative DNB editions/translations as predictors.
7. Runs robustness checks (influence diagnostics, trend comparisons with/without the final observed year) on the key time-series results.

## Outputs

### `analyses/`
Per-text and per-year statistics: author/text frequency summaries, metapopulation colonisation/extinction rates, turnover time series, Shannon diversity, contagion classification, survival "spells" data, Cox model coefficients, and pre-endpoint robustness diagnostics.

### `img/`
- `map_states.png` — Abitur text coverage by federal state (East Germany hatched).
- `heatmap_data.png` — state × year data availability.
- `hist_DNB_editions.png`, `hist_DNB_translations.png`, `scatter_DNB.png` — DNB edition/translation distributions and their correlation.
- `scatter_DNBxAbi.png` — Abitur coverage vs. DNB coverage.
- `author_heatmap.png`, `text_heatmap.png` — canon presence over time for recurring authors/texts.
- `gender_proportion.png` — share of female authors over time.
- `turnover_time_series.png`, `shannon_diversity_by_year.png`, `period_rates.png`, `period_contagion.png` — corpus-level dynamics over time.
- `km_survival_curve.png` — Kaplan–Meier curve for canon-membership "spells".
